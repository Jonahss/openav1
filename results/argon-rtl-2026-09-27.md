# Argon conformance through the RTL, first pass (2026-09-27, sexica, model-free mode)

Subset: Argon `profile0_core`, intra-only, no super-resolution, no film grain: **52 streams** (`tools/argon_index.py`).
Method: `TS_NOMODEL=1` in `tb/test_dec_top.py`: the full RTL pipeline (tile syntax → reconstruction → deblocking →
CDEF → loop restoration) decodes every frame and the output is compared byte for byte with dav1d's picture
(`DAV1D_EXTRA='--filmgrain 0'`). The frame buffer size is a Verilator parameter (`FBX`/`FBY`, log2 of width/height),
so the streams were batched by the smallest buffer that holds them.

| batch (buffer) | streams | identical frames | stopped by |
|---|---|---|---|
| small (1024x512, default) | 11 | 15 (2045, 3399 x6, 6312, 647, 7771 x5, 9404) + 13 (10407 x5, 2602 x5, 2650, 8304 x2) | 4592 f0, 8304 f2: intrabc |
| fb11x9 (2048x512) | 1 | 1 (20004) | — |
| fb12x9 (4096x512) | 6 | 5 (1597, 5257 x4) + 2 (707, 8243) | 5297 f0, 5366 f0: intrabc |
| fb12x12 (4096x4096) | 1 | 0 | 10455 f0: intrabc |

**Result: 36 frames across 14 streams are byte-identical to dav1d; 0 pixel mismatches.** Every stop is the RTL's
`unsupported` flag on a block with `use_intrabc = 1` (verified from the frame headers: all five streams have
`allow_intrabc = 1` on the stopping frame; 8304 frame 1 also allows it but no block uses it and it decoded identically).
The remaining 33 streams of the subset need frame buffers larger than 4096x512 (e.g. 23x3456, 2512x2470) or many
frames, and are deferred until the throughput work (the simulation runs at ~10k cycles/s on the Pi, ~3x slower
on sexica per stream).

Next: intra block copy (the sketch in the architecture note: an inter-style predictor reading the current frame's
pre-filter pixels), then the large-buffer classes, then super-resolution and film grain for the rest of profile0_core.

Logs: `logs/sx_argon_small*.out`, `logs/sx_argon_fb*.out` on sexica.
